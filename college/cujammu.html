🎓 Establishment

Founded on August 8, 2011, under the Central Universities Act, 2009.

Campus: Sprawling 610-acre campus in Samba district.

✅ Accreditation

Recognized by UGC.

NAAC 'B++' Grade accredited institution.

📘 Multidisciplinary Programs

  • Engineering: B.Tech, M.Tech in CSE and ECE.
  • Business Studies: MBA and Management courses.
  • Life Sciences: Integrated B.Sc. + M.Sc. courses.
  • Humanities: Social Sciences, Languages.
  • Others: Mass Communication, Security Studies.

💰 Fees Structure

UG Programs: ~₹20,000 (B.Voc) to higher for professional courses.

PG Programs: Economical fees, starting from ~₹19,560 for MA.

🏢 Modern Facilities

  • State-of-the-art specialized laboratories.
  • Central Library: Fully automated with RFID system.
  • Vibrant student services, workshops, and cultural events.

📅 Admissions

Primary selection mode: CUET (Central Universities Common Entrance Test) for UG.

Entrance exams for PG and Doctoral programs as per guidelines.

📍 Location

Rahya – Suchani (Bagla), Samba District, Jammu & Kashmir - 181143.
